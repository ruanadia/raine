# Privacy Policy — raine

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

**raine** is a personal, non-commercial tool that connects my own Pinterest account to an AI assistant (Claude) through a local MCP server. It is used only by its author.

## What data is accessed
With my authorization (Pinterest OAuth), the app reads and organizes **my own** Pinterest data: my profile, boards, board sections and Pins. It does not access other users' private data.

## How data is used
Data is used only to answer my requests (for example, listing my boards or moving one of my Pins to another board). It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## Storage and sharing
- The app runs locally on my own computer. Pinterest data is not stored in any database or server operated by this app.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on my computer and are never shared.
- Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Content I ask the AI assistant to process is sent to that assistant (Anthropic's Claude) only to carry out my request.

## Revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings → Security and logins → Apps, or by deleting the local tokens.
